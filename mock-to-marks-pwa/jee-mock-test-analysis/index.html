How to analyse your next JEE mock

Log every attempted question.

Do not just count correct and incorrect. Mark whether each question was correct, wrong, slow, guessed or left.

Tag the primary reason for every lost mark.

Choose one main cause. Avoid vague labels like careless mistake.

Rank causes by recoverable marks.

A weakness that costs 40 marks deserves more attention than one that costs 4.

Turn the top cause into the next 7-14 days.
