Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Mock-to-Marks is a mock-test diagnosis and study-planning tool built by ProDJEE, operated as an individual business. This page explains what information we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to everyone who uses the Mock-to-Marks web app at prodjee.in, whether or not you've signed in or paid.

2. What we collect

  • Account information — when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google. We use this only to identify your account; we never see or store your Google password.
  • Mock-test and plan data — the exam results, mistake patterns, and study-plan progress you enter into the app, linked to your account so your history and plan persist across devices.
  • Payment status — when you unlock the paid plan, our payment processor (Razorpay) handles your card/UPI/bank details directly. We never see or store this information ourselves — we only retain the payment status and a transaction reference, so we can confirm your access and process refunds if needed.
  • Local device data — some app state (like an in-progress mock you haven't finished) is kept only in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device.

3. How we use it

  • To generate your mistake diagnosis and 14-day practice plan.
  • To confirm which features you're entitled to (free vs. paid).
  • To respond if you contact us for support or a refund.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary data with the services that run the app, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Google — for sign-in.
  • Razorpay — for payment processing.

We do not share your data with anyone else, and we do not sell it.

5. Where it's stored

Account and mock-test data is stored on Google's Firebase infrastructure with standard access controls limiting who can read it. Only you, and the systems needed to run the app, can access your data.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email prodjeelabs@gmail.com from your registered email address. We'll confirm and complete deletion within a reasonable time, except where we're required to keep transaction records for legal or accounting purposes.

7. If you're under 18

Many of our users are students preparing for competitive exams and may be minors. We collect no more personal information than needed to run the service. A parent or guardian is welcome to contact us at any time about a minor's account or data.

Have a question about your data, or want it deleted? Just email prodjeelabs@gmail.com — that inbox is our grievance/privacy contact.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to access, correct, or erase your personal data, and to know how it's being used. Contact us using the email above to exercise any of these rights.

9. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember app state between visits. We don't use third-party advertising trackers.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.
