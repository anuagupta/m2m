Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

ProDJEE (operated as an individual business) runs prodjee.in, which includes Arena (JEE/NEET previous-year-question practice) and Mock-to-Marks (mock-test diagnosis and study planning). This policy explains what information we handle, where it lives and what you can do about it. Our design principle is simple: your study data stays with you — on your device and in your own Google Drive — not on our servers.

1. Who this covers

Everyone who uses prodjee.in or the installed ProDJEE app, including Arena and Mock-to-Marks.

2. What we collect and where it is stored

  • Google account information — when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile photo. We use them to identify your account, greet you by name and show your photo in the app. We never see your Google password.
  • Study data — your Arena progress (Gyan Points, streaks, answers, Mistake Vault, settings) and your Mock-to-Marks entries (mock results, mistake analysis, plans, profile preferences). This is stored only (a) in your browser’s local storage on your device, and (b) if you allow it, in a private, hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (see section 4). It is not sent to or stored on ProDJEE’s servers.
  • Consent record — the date you confirmed you are 18+ or have a parent/guardian’s permission and accepted these policies. Stored with your study data (device + your Drive).
  • Payment and entitlement records — when you subscribe to the Mock-to-Marks plan, Razorpay processes your card/UPI/bank details directly; we never see them. We keep your subscription status, its expiry date and order/transaction reference on Google Firebase, so we can confirm access.
  • How you found us — if you arrive through a tagged link, we may store that source (e.g. a campaign name) against your account in Firebase to understand which channels work.
  • Usage analytics — we use Google Analytics 4 and Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate usage (pages visited, device type, approximate location, referrer). Google Analytics uses cookies. We do not use advertising or remarketing features.

3. How we use it

  • To run Arena and Mock-to-Marks, generate your diagnosis and plan, and show your progress.
  • To back up and restore your study data through your Google Drive.
  • To confirm which features you are entitled to (free vs. paid).
  • To improve the product using aggregate analytics.
  • To respond when you contact us.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not build marketing profiles.

4. Google Drive backup and Google API data

With your permission, ProDJEE uses the Google Drive permission drive.appdata to save one file (prodjee-data.json) in the hidden “application data” folder of your Google Drive. This permission only lets ProDJEE see files that ProDJEE itself created there; we cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files. The file is read and written directly between your browser and Google; it does not pass through ProDJEE’s servers.

You can refuse this permission and still use ProDJEE (your data then stays only on your device), turn it on later from your Profile, or revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To remove the backup file yourself: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → ProDJEE → Delete hidden app data.

ProDJEE’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide the features described here, do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide those features or as required by law, do not use it for advertising, and do not allow humans to read it except with your consent, for security reasons, or as required by law.

5. Who we share data with

  • Google — sign-in (Firebase Authentication), your Drive backup, Firebase storage of payment/entitlement records, and Google Analytics.
  • Razorpay — payment processing.
  • Vercel — website hosting and privacy-friendly analytics.

Each processes data under its own privacy policy. We share nothing else with anyone, except where required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Study data stays on your device and in your Drive until you delete it. Use Profile → Delete my data to erase it from this device and from your Drive backup in one step, or Download my data to get a copy.
  • Payment and entitlement records are kept for as long as needed for your access and legal/accounting obligations.
  • To close your account and erase the Firebase records we are not legally required to keep, email prodjeelabs@gmail.com from your registered email address.

7. Children and students under 18

Most of our users are JEE/NEET aspirants, many under 18. Before first use, each user must confirm that they are 18 or older or that a parent or guardian has read our Terms and this Policy and given permission. We collect only what is needed to run the service and never use a child’s data for tracking, profiling or targeted advertising. Parents and guardians can contact us at any time to review or delete a child’s data.

8. Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask to access, correct, update or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate someone to exercise your rights, and raise a grievance. Most study data you can manage yourself in the app; for anything else, email us. If you are not satisfied with our response you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Security

Data moves over encrypted HTTPS connections. Your Drive backup is protected by your Google account. Please keep your Google account and phone secure; anyone with access to your unlocked device can see data stored on it.

10. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in, hold your study data and remember settings. Google Analytics sets cookies to measure aggregate usage. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and, where required, ask for your consent again in the app.

Grievance / privacy contact: prodjeelabs@gmail.com. We aim to acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 30 days.
